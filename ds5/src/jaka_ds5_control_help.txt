jaka-ds5-control - DualSense gamepad control for the JAKA arm

Not implemented yet. This binary will read a DualSense gamepad and stream
move and rotate commands to the jaka-cli serve protocol.

Planned mapping:
  Left stick     Move the head in the base XY plane
  Right stick    Rotate the head
  L2 / R2        Move down / up
  Cross          Stop, release to the neutral position
  Circle         Reset to the default pose
  PS button      Power on
  Touchpad       Power off

Usage:
  jaka-ds5-control [options]

Options:
  --ip <IP>       jaka-cli serve address, defaults to 127.0.0.1
  --port <port>   jaka-cli serve port, defaults to 9000
