jaka-ds5-control - DualSense gamepad control for the JAKA arm

Reads a DualSense gamepad and streams velocity commands to the jaka-cli
serve protocol over TCP. This binary is a pure protocol layer, it never
links the JAKA SDK. The robot moves smoothly, the sticks map to velocities.

Controls:
  Left stick     Move the head in the base XY plane, up to 100 mm/s
  L1 / R1        Move down / up at 40 mm/s
  Right stick    Tilt the head (up down) and turn it (left right), up to 20 deg/s
  Cross          Clear the e-stop
  Triangle       Reset to the default pose
  D-pad up       Power on
  D-pad down     Power off

  All inputs merge into one velocity command, so moving, lifting and
  rotating can happen at the same time.

Usage:
  jaka-ds5-control [options]

Options:
  --ip <IP>       jaka-cli serve address, defaults to 127.0.0.1
  --port <port>   jaka-cli serve port, defaults to 5533
